Planning Your Visit

What to Expect at Alive Faith

Here is how our ushers, ministers, and congregation prepare for your arrival.

Warm Hospitality

Our protocol team welcomes you with love, helps you find comfortable seating, and answers any questions.

Children & Teens Church

Dedicated age-appropriate spiritual instruction, secure care, and engaging lessons for your little ones.

Spirit-Filled Worship

An atmosphere filled with passionate adoration, thanksgiving, and prophetic praise that opens the heavens.

Revelatory Word

Uncompromised preaching of apostolic grace that builds unshakeable faith and manifests practical life answers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers for newcomers and first-time attendees visiting our sanctuary.

Come dressed comfortably and decently! Whether you prefer formal attire, smart casual, or traditional dress, you will feel completely at home. Our focus is on worshiping God in spirit and truth.
We recommend arriving 20 to 30 minutes before the scheduled time (around 5:30 PM on Fridays and 3:00 PM on Sundays). This allows ample time for parking, checking in children, and settling into prayer before corporate worship begins.
Our ministers and pastoral team are available right after each service at the front altar. You may also contact our pastoral line at +254 712 785 016 or submit a prayer request through this website for one-on-one appointments.
Yes, absolutely! We conduct regular water baptism services by full immersion and baby dedications during Sunday celebration services. Speak with the protocol desk or submit an inquiry note above to register for upcoming dates.
