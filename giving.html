Scripture Foundation

Kingdom Impact

Where Your Giving Goes

Every seed sown into Alive Faith Ministries bears direct, tangible fruit in lives and communities.

Soul Winning & Crusades

Funding open-air gospel crusades, regional evangelistic outreaches, and soul-winning literature across communities.

Discipleship & Training

Equipping ministers, producing teaching manuals, and establishing believer leadership foundations to mentor the next generation.

Compassion & Welfare

Providing food relief, educational support for vulnerable children, and emergency benevolent assistance to families in need.

Global Broadcast Reach

Transmitting high-definition live streams, podcasts, and digital media to reach unchurched believers worldwide.

Simple Guide

How to Give via M-Pesa

5 straightforward steps to transfer your offering directly from your mobile phone.

Step 01

Open M-Pesa

Go to the M-Pesa menu or SIM toolkit on your mobile phone.

Step 02

Select Send Money

Choose Send Money from your M-Pesa options.

Step 03

Enter Number

Enter 0728 838 977 (Alive Faith Ministries).

Step 04

Enter Amount

Enter any amount as purposed in your heart, with your name and purpose as reference.

Step 05

Confirm with PIN

Enter your M-Pesa PIN and press send to complete.
