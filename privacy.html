Privacy Policy

Sanctity, Integrity, and Pastoral Confidentiality of Your Personal Information

Effective: October 2026 • Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 Compliant • Alive Faith Ministries Legal Desk

1. Introduction and Ministry Commitment

Alive Faith Ministries (referred to as "AFM", "the Ministry", "we", or "our") is a non-denominational Christian ministry with a priestly mandate to raise believers who know and trust God for the realization of their fullness in Christ Jesus (Ephesians 2:8-10).

We hold the privacy and personal trust of our congregation, visitors, and online fellowship in the highest reverence. This Privacy Policy outlines transparently how we collect, handle, safeguard, and respect your personal information across our website (www.livefaithministries.org) and digital ministry platforms, in accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and recognized international privacy standards.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily choose to share with us:

  • Contact and Visitor Planning: Your full name, email address, phone number, and intended visit date when planning a sanctuary visit or sending a message.
  • Prayer Requests and Salvation Decisions: Prayer points, health intercession petitions, family matters, and salvation testimonies submitted to our pastoral team.
  • Voluntary Giving Verification: M-Pesa transaction reference codes, donation purposes (such as Tithe, Sanctuary Building, Covenant Partnership, or Charity), and contribution amounts entered voluntarily. We never request, capture, or store sensitive banking PINs or credit card CVV codes.
  • Technical and Security Metadata: Internet Protocol (IP) addresses collected solely for firewall rate-limiting and anti-bot mitigation, and essential session identifiers ensuring secure communication.
Sacred Pastoral Confidentiality: Prayer requests, counseling notes, and spiritual questions are treated with the highest sacred pastoral confidence. They are shared solely with authorized ministry prayer intercessors and pastoral staff for spiritual agreement. We will never sell, lease, or distribute your prayer petitions to commercial entities or external third parties.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used strictly for legitimate spiritual and administrative ministry purposes:

  • Standing with you in prayer and providing pastoral follow-up.
  • Welcoming first-time sanctuary guests and assisting with transport or service directions.
  • Acknowledging voluntary contributions and providing covenant partnership updates.
  • Maintaining digital platform security, mitigating automated spam, and protecting visitors and ministry services.

4. Cookies and Technical Data

Our website uses lightweight, essential cookies to preserve session continuity, enforce Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) defenses, and keep your browsing experience smooth and safe. We do not use third-party tracking pixels or commercial ad targeting networks.

5. Your Data Protection Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and relevant data protection principles, you possess the following rights:

  • Right of Access: You may request confirmation of any personal information we hold concerning you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request the correction of outdated or inaccurate contact details.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request the deletion of your contact records or submitted message history.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your consent for email updates or phone communications at any time.

6. Security and Safeguards

We implement defense-in-depth security measures, including HTTPS encryption in transit, strict Content Security Policies, parameterized database protocols, and restricted staff access to preserve the integrity and confidentiality of your information.
