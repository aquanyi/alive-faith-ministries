About Alive Faith Ministries

A non-denominational Ministry with a heavenly mandate to raise believers who know, trust, and manifest the fullness of God in Christ Jesus.

Our Divine Calling

Raised for Fullness in Christ

Experiencing uncompromised faith, transformative grace, and intimate fellowship with the Father.

Alive Faith Ministries was birthed out of a relentless heavenly burden to see sons and daughters of God awakened to their true priestly stature. In a world full of religious conformity and spiritual weariness, our calling is to unveil the unsearchable riches of Christ Jesus, equipping every believer to walk in experiential knowledge, divine authority, and unshakable trust in God.

We believe that salvation is not merely a ticket to heaven, but an immediate introduction into the supernatural life of God on earth. Through revelatory preaching of the Word, fervent intercession, and sincere worship, we cultivate an altar where believers transition from seeking God for things to seeking God for who He is.

Biblical Doctrine

Statement of Faith

The unshakeable biblical truths that anchor our doctrine, teaching, prayer life, and walk in Christ Jesus.

Foundational Truths

10 Articles of Faith

Search doctrines or filter through our scriptural convictions below.

Showing all 10 foundational articles of faith
I

God the Creator

We believe in God the creator of heaven and earth, the Supreme Being and Father of us all.

Scriptural Proof:
Genesis 1:1, 26 • Deuteronomy 6:4 • 1 John 5:7 • Matthew 28:19: "For there are three that bear record in heaven, the Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost: and these three are one."
II

Jesus Christ the Son

We believe Jesus Christ is His son who was God manifest in the flesh; He walked the earth, lived among men; died and was raised again to life.

Scriptural Proof:
John 1:1, 14 • 1 Corinthians 15:3, 4 • 1 Timothy 3:16 • Hebrews 4:15: "And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, and we beheld His glory."
III

Creation & The Fall of Man

We believe that man is a created being, made in the likeness and image of God, but through Adam’s transgression and fall, sin came into the world.

Scriptural Proof:
Ephesians 2:8, 9 • Romans 3:24 • Romans 5:8 • Titus 3:5: "For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: not of works."
IV

Redemption & Divine Healing

We believe that the redemptive work of Christ on the cross provides salvation for the soul of everyone that believes and divine healing for the body.

Scriptural Proof:
1 John 1:9 • Acts 3:19 • 2 Corinthians 7:10 • Luke 13:3: "If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness."
V

Freedom from Sin & Holiness

We believe in the freedom of the Christian from sin, and the consequent blessing of being bond servants of Christ; and that it exhibits our fruit unto holiness and life everlasting (Romans 6:22).

Scriptural Proof:
Romans 5:1 • Galatians 2:16 • Romans 8:1 • 2 Corinthians 5:21: "Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ."
VI

Justification by Faith

We believe that we are justified by faith, and have received of the free gift of righteousness through God’s Grace, which gift is not of works (Galatians 2:16, Romans 4:16).

Scriptural Proof:
Matthew 28:19 • Acts 2:38 • Romans 6:3, 4 • Colossians 2:12: "Buried with Him in baptism, wherein also ye are risen with Him through the faith of the operation of God."
VII

God's Purpose & Work in Us

We believe that God wills and works in men and that His purposes are fulfilled by His work through us who believe.

Scriptural Proof:
Acts 1:8 • Acts 2:4 • Matthew 3:11 • Luke 24:49: "And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance."
VIII

The Word of God as Absolute Truth

We believe that the Word of God is the sole standard of truth and is the pattern for Christian living.

Scriptural Proof:
1 Corinthians 12:4 to 11 • Romans 12:6 to 8 • 1 Corinthians 14:1: "Now concerning spiritual gifts, brethren, I would not have you ignorant... but the manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man to profit withal."
IX

The Holy Spirit & Divine Enablement

We believe that when an individual receives the Holy Spirit, he/she receives divine enablement for Christian service and witness.

Scriptural Proof:
1 Thessalonians 5:23 • Hebrews 12:14 • 1 Peter 1:15, 16 • 2 Corinthians 7:1: "And the very God of peace sanctify you wholly; and I pray God your whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless."
X

The Second Coming of Christ

We believe that Jesus will return and “. . . The dead in Christ shall rise first: Then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air . . .” (1 Thess. 4:16–17).

Scriptural Proof:
1 Thessalonians 4:16, 17 • 1 Corinthians 15:51, 52 • Titus 2:13 • Revelation 22:20: "For the Lord Himself shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God: and the dead in Christ shall rise first."
The Roman Road

The Decision to Receive Christ

Salvation is God's free gift to all mankind. You can receive Jesus Christ right now where you are.

Step 01
The Need

Step 02
The Free Gift
Romans 6:23

The wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Step 03
The Confession
Romans 10:9, 10

Confess with your mouth the Lord Jesus and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead.

Step 04
The Assurance
John 5:24

Whoever hears His Word and believes Him that sent Him has everlasting life and shall not come into condemnation.
